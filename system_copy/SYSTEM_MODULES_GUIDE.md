# NBC SACCOS SYSTEM MODULES GUIDE

## Core Banking System - Module Descriptions

### 1. Dashboard
Central command center providing real-time overview of all critical business metrics, including member statistics, loan portfolios, savings balances, transaction volumes, and key performance indicators. Offers quick access to frequently used functions and alerts for pending actions.

### 2. Branches
Comprehensive branch management system for handling multiple branch locations. Manage branch details, assign staff, track branch-specific performance metrics, configure branch-level settings, and monitor inter-branch transactions and operations.

### 3. Members
Complete member registry and management system. Maintain member profiles, contact information, identification documents, membership status, registration details, and member relationships. Track member lifecycle from onboarding to exit.

### 4. Shares
Shareholder capital management module for tracking share purchases, transfers, withdrawals, and dividends. Manage share certificates, calculate member equity, process share transactions, and generate shareholder statements and ownership reports.

### 5. Savings
Multi-product savings account management supporting various account types (regular savings, fixed deposits, children accounts, etc.). Handle deposits, withdrawals, interest calculations, account opening/closure, and savings product configuration.

### 6. Deposits
Transaction processing and history tracking for all deposit activities. Record cash deposits, check deposits, mobile deposits, and automated deposits. Maintain detailed transaction logs with timestamps, teller information, and supporting documentation.

### 7. Loans
End-to-end loan management system covering loan applications, appraisals, disbursements, repayments, restructuring, and collections. Support multiple loan products, collateral management, guarantor tracking, interest calculations, penalty management, and loan portfolio analysis.

### 8. Accounting
Double-entry general ledger accounting system with chart of accounts, journal entries, trial balance, financial statements (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow), and accounting period management. Ensures financial accuracy and regulatory compliance.

### 9. Products Management
Product catalog administration for configuring all financial products offered by the SACCO. Define product terms, interest rates, fees, eligibility criteria, limits, and approval workflows for savings, loans, and investment products.

### 10. Payments
Payment processing hub for both incoming and outgoing payments. Handle member payments, vendor payments, salary disbursements, utility payments, third-party transfers, and payment reconciliation. Integration with payment gateways and mobile money platforms.

### 11. Human Resources
Complete HR management system for staff administration including employee records, contracts, leave management, attendance tracking, payroll processing, performance evaluations, training records, and organizational structure management.

### 12. Cash Management
Treasury and cash operations management covering till operations, vault management, cash transfers between branches, cash-in-hand tracking, daily cash counts, cash float management, and cash forecasting for liquidity planning.

### 13. Reconciliation
Account reconciliation tools for balancing internal accounts, bank reconciliations, inter-branch reconciliations, suspense account clearing, and identifying discrepancies. Automated matching of transactions with supporting documentation.

### 14. Reports Manager
Comprehensive reporting engine with pre-built and custom report templates. Generate operational reports, financial statements, regulatory reports, management reports, audit trails, and ad-hoc reports with flexible date ranges and filters.

### 15. Billing
Billing and invoicing system for managing recurring charges, service fees, loan fees, penalty charges, and member billing. Automate billing cycles, generate invoices, track payment status, and handle billing disputes.

### 16. Expenses
Expense tracking and management for all operational costs. Record and categorize expenses, manage expense approvals, track vendor payments, handle expense reimbursements, and generate expense reports by category, department, or time period.

### 17. Budget Management
Financial planning and budget control system. Create annual budgets, allocate funds to departments/branches, track budget utilization vs. actuals, monitor variances, and adjust budget allocations. Support multi-year budget planning and forecasting.

### 18. Institution Settings
System-wide configuration and settings management. Configure organizational details, business rules, system parameters, regional settings (currency, timezone, language), security policies, workflow approvals, and integration settings.

### 19. Transactions
Universal transaction ledger providing comprehensive view of all financial activities across the system. Search, filter, and analyze transactions by type, date, member, account, branch, or amount. Export transaction data for analysis and reporting.

### 20. Self Services
Member self-service portal enabling members to access account information, view statements, transfer funds, apply for loans, update personal details, and access e-services without visiting branch offices. Enhances member experience and reduces operational workload.

### 21. Active Loans
Focused dashboard for monitoring all current active loan accounts. Track loan performance, upcoming repayments, overdue loans, early settlement requests, loan restructuring cases, and portfolio health metrics. Quick access to loan servicing functions.

### 22. Mobile & Web Portal
Configuration and management interface for member-facing digital channels. Manage portal content, configure available services, set transaction limits, customize portal appearance, monitor portal usage, and manage digital banking features.

### 23. Email
Email communication management system for sending automated notifications, statements, alerts, and marketing communications to members. Configure email templates, manage email queues, track delivery status, and maintain email communication logs.

### 24. Subscriptions
Manage system subscription plans, licensing, feature access levels, and add-on services. Track subscription status, renewal dates, payment history, and feature entitlements. Configure tiered access based on subscription level.

### 25. Approvals
Centralized approval workflow management for transactions and operations requiring authorization. Define approval hierarchies, set approval limits by role, track pending approvals, maintain approval audit trails, and configure multi-level approval processes.

### 26. Users Manager
User account administration for system access control. Create and manage user accounts, assign roles and permissions, set password policies, manage user groups, track user activity, handle user deactivation, and configure two-factor authentication.
